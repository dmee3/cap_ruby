Read the lead carefully

The discount is not the same lever

2025 & 2026 — optional code

List price stayed at $115 Music / $90 Visual all season. A 15%-off promo code (15FOR15, CC25MUSAUD) was optional and had to be typed in.

In 2026 it accounted for 55% of paid registrations; in 2025, section-specific codes filled a similar role. Usage stopped ~5 days out.

2027 — automatic sale price

The current price is the sale: $100 Music / $80 Visual, applied to everyone with no code. It ends tomorrow and prices rise the next day.

All 82 paid registrations so far are at this price. This is the same structure as 2023, when a $75 sale price stepped up to $100 late in the cycle — so 2023 is the closest guide to what happens next.

What to take from this

Bottom line

  1. The lead is real and consistent. 2027 has led at every checkpoint from 40 days out — it’s a faster season, not one late burst.
  2. The sale deadline is pulling demand forward. A no-code discount ending tomorrow gives fence-sitters a hard reason to register now — expect a spike, then a sharp slowdown.
  3. The lead is broad, but cymbals lags. Every section is ahead of both prior seasons; only cymbals (5 vs. 15 & 19) needs a targeted push.
  4. A real price step flattens pace hard. In 2023 — the last season with this same sale structure — registrations dropped to ~2/day for two weeks after the step, before the usual final-3-days rush.
  5. Projection: ~145–170 final, based on 2023’s post-step pace. Above 2024 (127), 2026 (128), near 2025 (154). Earlier optimism assumed a soft discount, not this cliff.
