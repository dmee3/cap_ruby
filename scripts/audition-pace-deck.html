Cymbals is still the soft spot

7 cymbal registrations so far vs. 15 and 19 the last two full seasons. It has added only two since the 20-day mark. Battery overall is ahead, so this line is genuinely lagging — it is the one targeted push worth making.

Battery registers late — normally

In 2025, battery went from 48 at 10 days out to 100 by audition day. 2027 sits at 58, but its late surge has to happen at full price rather than into a live discount.

Read the lead carefully

The discount is not the same lever

2025 & 2026 — optional code

List price stayed at $115 Music / $90 Visual all season. A 15%-off promo code (15FOR15, CC25MUSAUD) was optional and had to be typed in.

In 2026 it accounted for 55% of paid registrations; in 2025, section-specific codes filled a similar role. Usage stopped ~5 days out.

2027 — automatic sale price, now expired

The sale price was the list price: $100 Music / $80 Visual, applied to everyone with no code. It ended 8 Sep; every order since pays $115 / $90.

93 registrations came in at the sale price, 9 at full price since. Daily pace fell from 4.0/day to 1.0/day — a quarter of the pre-step rate, steeper than the ~2/day that 2023 suggested.

What to take from this

Bottom line

  1. The lead held, but it is narrowing. 2027 leads at every checkpoint — 102 vs. 81 and 80 — but the margin has fallen from +63% at 20 days out to +28% today.
  2. The deadline pulled demand forward, exactly as predicted. Week 3 spiked to 34 registrations, the highest single week of any season, then week 2 collapsed to 5.
  3. The price step bit harder than 2023 implied. Pace fell from 4.0/day to 1.0/day — a quarter of the prior rate, not the ~2/day the 2023 analogue forecast.
  4. The lead is broad, but cymbals still lags. Every section is ahead of both prior seasons; cymbals (7 vs. 15 & 19) has added only two in ten days and is the one line worth a push.
  5. Revised projection: ~125–150 final, down from the ~145–170 called at 20 days out. Prior seasons booked 37–47% of their final in the last ten days, but both still had a discount live; at the observed 1.0/day plus a normal final-3-day rush, 2027 lands near 2026 (128) and below 2025 (154).
